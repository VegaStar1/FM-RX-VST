FM RX is installed

Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins (Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig, FL Studio — any host that supports VST3). FM RX appears as an effect from NUWMUN.

A short guide opens on first launch; the full manual is in /Library/Application Support/NUWMUN/FM RX and behind the gear button → Help → Full manual.
